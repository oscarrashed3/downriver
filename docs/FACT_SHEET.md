# Science claims to review before release

Each claim appears in the game's dialogue, journal or systems. Flag anything that needs correcting.

| # | Claim | Where | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Snowpack acts as natural water storage: it fills in winter and melts gradually, feeding rivers through summer. | Ysolde, journal "The mountain's water jar" | Standard hydrology; snowmelt-dominated rivers peak in late spring and sustain summer flow. |
| 2 | Less snowpack means lower river flow downstream, especially late in summer. | Ysolde, ripple system | Widely documented in snowmelt basins. |
| 3 | Wind moves snow from windward to leeward slopes, loading the leeward side and raising avalanche risk. | Bram, journal "Wind moves snow" | Standard avalanche science (wind loading). |
| 4 | Snow fences slow wind so it deposits snow behind them; placement relative to wind matters. | Puzzle, journal "Fences that catch snow" | Used on highways and in water harvesting. |
| 5 | Ice stupas (artificial glaciers) store winter water as ice cones that melt in spring when water is scarce; their cone shape reduces melt. | Ysolde, journal "Towers of ice" | Developed in Ladakh, India. |
| 6 | Long-term measurements show trends that single readings cannot. | Tor, journal "What the poles say" | Basic scientific method. The pole numbers are invented for the game. |
| 7 | The water cycle: sea evaporates into clouds, clouds fall as snow and rain, rivers return water to the sea. | Cloud quest, finale | Standard. |
| 8 | Some pinecones open only after fire; small controlled burns reduce the risk of big fires. | Tamsin (sketch) | Serotinous cones; prescribed burning. |
| 9 | Roots hold soil; clearing forest for a single crop increases erosion and muddy rivers. | Oro (sketch) | Standard soil science. |
| 10 | Wetlands absorb floodwater; walls alone do not solve flooding. | Cassian (sketch) | Nature-based flood management. |
| 11 | Rising seas push salt water into wells and fields. | Maren (sketch) | Saltwater intrusion. |
